About Us

Dugnad is a contemporary, fast-growing school located in Kavule-Jomayi Estate along Matuga–Semuto Road in Wakiso District, just an hour’s drive from Kampala. We provide an activity-based curriculum that produces a holistic child through formal and informal early childhood learning.

Through a talented and experienced staff team with a low teacher-pupil ratio, we offer lifelong learning built on foundational skills and critical thinking.

Admission and Hours

Who we admit

Day care from six months, kindergarten from three years and primary from six years. A numeracy and English literacy test may be used to place a child in the right class.

What to bring

2 passport photos of the child, 2 passport photos of parents, copies of the child’s birth certificate and immunisation card.

Working hours

Monday to Friday. Kindergarten 7:30am – 12:30pm. Day care and primary 7:30am – 4:30pm. Late pick-up after 5:00pm attracts a fine of 5,000 UGX.

Parents’ role

Support school programmes, be a role model for learning, ensure consistent attendance and report your child’s behaviour and health to the school.
